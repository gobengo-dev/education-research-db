# Topic Ontology

## Purpose

Defines how OpenAlex topics are classified for harvesting and analysis.

---

## Current Categories

### Core Topics
Topics directly related to:
- education
- pedagogy
- curriculum
- assessment
- learning sciences
- educational psychology
- literacy
- schooling
- teacher education

### Adjacent Topics
Topics connected indirectly to education:
- developmental psychology
- cognitive science
- sociology
- language development
- STEM outreach

### Excluded Topics
Topics outside project scope.

---

## Planned Extensions

Potential future tagging dimensions:
- Early childhood
- Primary education
- Secondary education
- Higher education
- Vocational education
- Inclusive education
- Gifted education
- Indigenous education
- Educational technology
- Policy and leadership

---

## Important Design Principles

1. Topic inclusion should be reproducible.
2. Topic decisions should be documented.
3. Adjacent topics should remain distinguishable from core topics.
4. Topic structures should support future reclassification.
